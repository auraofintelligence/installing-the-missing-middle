# Installing the Missing Middle

A national community-compute and useful-heat installation proposal by Luke Nathan Hayes, prepared for joint development with Megan and Brad.

The project connects a national installation business with a federation of people and corporate vehicles, locally owned compute, paid workforce learning, personal Auras, civic simulations and community-selected applications.

## Direction

- Work backwards from 30 million people and one single-rack node per 10,000 people: a 3,000-node planning baseline.
- Invite participation across all postcodes in parallel; progress installations through the responses and relationships that develop.
- Brad builds the core infrastructure delivery team through his data-centre installation experience and industry network.
- Megan develops institutional relationships, contract management and commercial arrangements.
- Luke develops the integrated architecture, application programmes and federation connections.
- Finance deployment with government funding, finite-return investment and co-operative acquisition, while planning for changing compute generations.

The browser presentation, complete original-document library, supplied project links and agent handover briefs are being assembled in this repository.

The plan · Luke Nathan Hayes

A shared infrastructure for many useful lives

The uploaded programmes supply part of the workload base—not a closed catalogue of permitted uses.

Personal Auras and chosen AI tools

People develop their own personal Auras across devices and services, retaining their data, corrections and digital twins. Workloads include retrieval, personal-model development, inference, specialist agents, media processing, memory palaces and chosen device connections. Ordinary screens, voice, lists and maps remain available alongside XR. [D04, D05, D07]

Care across a changing life

Adaptive whole-of-life support connects younger-onset dementia, disability support, ageing, changes of home and changes of care provider. Meaningful media, spatial memory palaces, accessible interfaces and relevant personal context support the intended continuity. The personal Aura remains connected to the person’s wider life, not confined to one service category. [D06]

Children, young people and education

The children-and-teens proposal uses Verse Box and supported everyday learning: creative work, self-understanding, legal relevance, mentoring and a continuing record through moves and transitions. It connects into opportunities for adulthood and small school pilots. This is a distinct pathway from the adult hyperbaric programme. [D03]

Tourism, culture and civic participation

Tourism and domestic travel develop the qualified self through music, sport, culture, nature and real encounters. Workloads connect chosen observations, personal reflection, authorised place information, immersive media, operators and continuing learning. Volunteer development and the Brisbane 2032 horizon give the programme further settings for participation. [D07]

Council and electorate simulations

Local, state and federal geographic twins connect places, infrastructure, services and possible changes. Communities can examine scenarios, compare assumptions and investigate consequences before committing resources. Personal Aura records remain within their own permissions; participation in a place model does not transfer ownership of a person’s private archive. [D04, D11; current direction]

Wellbeing, Genesis and scientific exploration

Wellbeing and longevity hubs add personal-model development, session-media processing, instrument analysis, research and materials simulation. The Global Sensorium adds Earth and space modelling, sensor data, reproducible experiments and side-by-side comparison of established and unconventional hypotheses. Communities can develop their own models and questions rather than receiving a single imposed account. [D04, D14]

Creative, business and community work

Media production, local enterprise, technical design, language tools, environmental observation and community-selected applications sit alongside these programmes. Geode Development page 37 explicitly joins ready SET, wellbeing hubs, community twins, media, instrument analysis and materials simulation. Each participating service supplies its workload and resource brief. [D04, p. 37]
